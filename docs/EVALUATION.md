# Evaluation design

Practice is adaptive and permits three progressive hints. Fixed checks use ten items, two per family, all at designed level 2. Forms A/B are deterministically selected from disjoint canonical structures and native configurations. Manifest version `balanced-1` includes the concrete frozen tasks, source/adapter versions, difficulty, form, prior releases, repeat-form flag, and feedback policy. They are matched by configuration, not psychometrically equated. Assessment generation happens before any answer and does not follow the adaptive policy.

Practice eligibility uses a stable structural hash partition. Course graphs are invariant to node relabeling and logical formulas to role renaming. Native induction requires all surviving candidates to agree on the target output. Interactive hypotheses share the finite grammar; query outputs, not hidden rule metadata, are returned before submission. The native split also uses structural fingerprints and bounded retries. Exact native task fingerprints already encountered by a guest are excluded from fresh practice issuance.

The exploratory probe uses six level-2 relational-ordering items in a scheduling presentation withheld from ordinary practice and onboarding. Its declared outcome is related-task **presentation generalization**. It does not claim far transfer, an untrained family, reserved-composition generalization, or a latent intelligence change. Prerequisites and query controls remain available; task-specific strategies, hints and AI stay suppressed.

During an unfinished assessment, even correctness is sealed. At completion only aggregate outcomes appear in Progress. Per-item correctness, solutions, review and AI require the explicit release action. All these checks are enforced in backend views, journal, export, and discussion, not just hidden controls. Releases are logged; later forms carry prior-release flags. Checks after two form assignments are explicitly marked repeated, since infinite matched alternate forms are not established.

Practice accuracy is shown with independent denominators by family and level, and assisted counts stay separate. Delayed review includes only unaided attempts at least 24 hours after the linked original. Optional confidence is collected before feedback; Brier score is mean squared prediction error with a sample count. Restored JSON is editable, so restored outcomes are segregated and cannot enter canonical practice/check statistics or advance the scheduler.

No IQ, brain age, normative percentile, empirical cognitive-gain number, or invented external assessment is calculated. Better practiced performance, delayed retention, reserved-presentation transfer, untrained-family performance and independent psychometric assessment are distinct outcomes. Only the first three are supported as MVP outcomes; the built-in probe's matching is exploratory. To study broader transfer, add independently licensed assessments and a preregistered experiment with active controls, delayed follow-up, adequate sample size and appropriate uncertainty estimates.

References: [Rodas et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-024-02466-8), [Callaway et al. 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8944825/), and [Reasoning Gym](https://github.com/open-thought/reasoning-gym). These motivate design choices and do not establish Human Fluid's efficacy.
