- Native ordering, induction and hypothesis tasks, lessons, SVG marks and explanations were authored for this application during implementation. They have automated formal verification, not a claim of independent human curriculum review or psychometric validation.

The bank contains 520 accepted imported items: 50 practice and 15 check per family per designed level. `content/bank-report.json` records the initial generation run. Subsequent reports are saved beside the persistent database. Generation is deterministic (source version, adapter version, seed, configuration) and stops after at most 20,000 candidates per family/level. Duplicate structures and invalid/source-disagreeing items are excluded with counted rejection reasons. Missing required counts fail setup/readiness; no toy-source fallback is provided. Re-run `make bank` to verify all witnesses.

Dependency fingerprints are exact canonical representations over in/out-degree partitions and permutations within those partitions. They include isolated nodes. Logic fingerprints enumerate every role renaming and normalize commutative connectives. Native ordering fingerprints combine canonical premise and claim-augmented graphs; conservative collisions may exclude additional items. Scalar/list fingerprints capture the stated grammar, hidden rule and shown evidence; hypothesis rule/grammar configurations are fingerprinted without query seeds. A stable fingerprint partition, rather than a random seed partition, isolates practice from fixed checks.

Four designed levels alter prerequisite interactions/cycle lengths; logical population/depth; partial-order complexity; and grammar/individual/list/composition operations. They are curriculum settings, not calibrated ability estimates. A fresh seed is never described as far transfer. Scheduling is a reserved ordering wrapper and is labeled only exploratory presentation transfer. Induction and hypothesis testing share a grammar, which is explicitly disclosed.

Research sources motivate the application but do not validate it. No assessment stimuli from MaRs-IB, ICAR, ARC, Callaway's datasets, or other externally licensed research material are redistributed. Relevant links and interpretation appear in About and `EVALUATION.md`.
